Player System

Privacy Policy · Effective 29 October 2026 · Developer: Nishant (NRRL), Aligarh, India

Player System is a habit and self-improvement game for Android. This policy explains what the app uses, why, where it is stored, and how to delete it.

1. Data stored only on your phone

Your quests, levels, stats, history, journal entries, mood logs, body check-ins, items and settings are stored on your device. They are not sent anywhere unless you turn on one of the optional online features below. Android's own device backup may include this data if you have it enabled on your phone.

2. Health Connect (optional)

If you grant permission, the app reads these Health Connect data types: Steps, Exercise sessions and Sleep sessions. They are used only to:

  • complete the Steps and Training quests automatically when you reach your target,
  • give a "well rested" bonus after 7 or more hours of sleep,
  • verify the Steps and Training quests for the friends ranking (if you use Friends).

Health data stays on your device. When you verify a quest for ranking, only the quest name, the day, and the step count or exercise minutes for that quest are sent to the app's server. Health data is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to build a profile for anyone else, and never shared with third parties.

The use of information received from Health Connect will adhere to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Screen-time check (optional)

If you grant Usage access, the app reads how many minutes you used Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X, Snapchat and Reddit today, to check the Discipline quest. App usage details stay on your device. When you verify the Discipline quest for ranking, only a result of "0 minutes after the cutoff" is sent.

4. Friends and ranking (optional)

If you go online in Friends, the app creates a hunter profile with a random ID, a 6-character hunter code and a secret key stored on your phone. The profile holds the name you choose, your level, rank, title, streak, ranking points, the quests you verified each day, and the friends you add. Your friends can see these. Cheers, nudges and duel challenges you send are stored so the recipient can see them. No email address, phone number, contacts, location or advertising ID is collected.

5. Cloud backup (optional)

If you turn on Cloud backup, a copy of your app data (including journal notes) is stored on the app's server so you can restore it on a new phone with your backup key. Anyone who has your backup key can restore your data, so keep it private.

6. Where data is stored and who processes it

Online features use a Supabase database hosted in Mumbai, India, accessed over encrypted HTTPS. Supabase acts only as a hosting provider. The app shows no ads and contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. Data is not sold or shared with anyone else.

7. Retention and deletion

  • In the app: Friends → "Leave the network" permanently deletes your online profile, friend links, messages, ranking history and cloud backup.
  • On the web: use the account deletion page with your backup key.
  • On your phone: Settings → Reset the System erases all local data; uninstalling the app also removes it.

Online data is kept until you delete it. Inactive data is not used for any other purpose.

8. Children

The app is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For questions or requests, use the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play listing.
