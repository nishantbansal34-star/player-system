Delete your account

Player System · by Nishant (NRRL)

Deleting your Player System online account permanently removes your hunter profile, name, friend links, cheers and duel messages, ranking points history and cloud backup from the app's server. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you reset the app or uninstall it.

Option 1: in the app

Open More → Friends, scroll down, tap Leave the network and tap again to confirm.

Option 2: on this page

Paste your backup key (shown in the app under Settings → Cloud backup → Show backup key; it looks like ABC123- followed by a long code).

If you no longer have the app or your key, contact the developer using the email on the Google Play listing with your player name and hunter code, and the account will be deleted within 30 days.
